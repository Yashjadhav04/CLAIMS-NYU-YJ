# Part D cost trend briefing (real CMS public data)

Generated by `python -m partd.briefing`. Gross cost before rebates, all of Medicare Part D. Not any one plan's data.

## Bottom line
Gross Part D cost per member per month rose from $341 (2020) to $442 (2024), then went flat from 2023 to 2024. Total spending reached $289B in 2024. The flat year hides two opposite forces: more claims per member and a drop in cost per claim, led by insulin list-price cuts.

## What I would tell leadership
1. **Cost per claim, not volume, drove 2020 to 2023.** Claims per member barely moved (2,556 to 2,596 per 1,000 member months); spend per claim went from $133 to $171.
2. **2024 was an exception.** Spend per claim fell to $169. Insulin explains about $8B of a $29B negative price effect; the remainder is spread across ~2,700 drugs and not yet explained.
3. **GLP-1 drugs are the growth story.** $7.2B to $27.9B between 2020 and 2024 (9.7% of spend).
4. **2026 is a reset for ten drugs.** They were $61B (21%) of 2024 spend. Applying the announced cuts to 2024 volume removes about $37B at list-price level. Early Q1 2026 data shows spend per claim already 30% to 71% lower for most of them.
5. **Geography matters.** Excluding DC (distorted by cross-border prescribing), NY is highest at $590 and WY lowest at $235 per member per month, a 2.5x spread. The data cannot say whether that is use, price, drug mix or plan mix.

## What to watch
- 2025 annual data when CMS publishes it; the preliminary quarterly figure ($351B) is not comparable to the annual series.
- Whether the early drop in spend per claim for the negotiated drugs holds as Q2 2026 and later quarters arrive.
- Mix of enrollment: MA-PD share rose from 47% to 57% of Part D enrollees between 2020 and 2024.

## Caveats
- Gross cost excludes rebates; net cost to a plan is lower and unknown here.
- Dose units for injectables can change with strength and packaging, so unit-price movements for insulin and GLP-1 drugs are partly definitional.
- State cost is by prescriber location; enrollment by member residence.
- The 2026 price cuts are announced prices against list; the data check is directional.

## Questions I would ask the team
- Which of these views do you already produce, and which are rebuilt by hand each quarter?
- How do you bridge gross to net in your own reporting, and which rebate assumptions are standard?
- Which negotiated-drug effects are already in the 2026 bid versus still to come?
